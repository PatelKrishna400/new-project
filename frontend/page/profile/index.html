How Tasks Work & How To Use

Watch the video tutorial and follow step-by-step guides to complete tasks, earn coins & unlock premium subscriptions!

📺 Official Video Tutorial
Watch on YouTube ↗
📋 Step-by-Step Task Walkthrough
STEP 1
Choose Simple or Premium Task
Simple Tasks (📋) are free for all players (earn 50 🪙 each). Premium Tasks (💎) give massive payouts (earn 200 🪙 each) for subscribed VIP members.
STEP 2
Click "GO" & View Mission Target
Tap the "GO" button to open the mission URL (YouTube video, Telegram channel, website, or sponsor link). Keep the page active for the required watch duration.
STEP 3
Click "VERIFY" & Win Instant Coins
Once your mission timer finishes, tap VERIFY. Anti-cheat validates your visit and instantly credits +50 🪙 or +200 🪙 directly to your live wallet balance!
STEP 4
How to Subscribe to Premium Tasks
Open the Premium Task tab on the Home page. Choose from 3 discounted plans: 15 Days (500 ⭐ • 10% OFF), 30 Days (1,000 ⭐ • 20% OFF), or 3 Months (2,000 ⭐ • 35% OFF) to unlock all VIP tasks.
ADMIN
For Admins: How to Create & Edit Tasks
In the Admin Panel → Edit Task → tap ＋ Add Mission. Select Simple or Premium, input title, coin bounty, target URL, and cooldown time, then click Save!
💳 How do I redeem Amazon Gift Cards? ▼

Navigate to the Wallet page in the bottom menu. Select your voucher: ₹1,000 (100,000 ⭐), ₹5,000 (500,000 ⭐), or ₹10,000 (1,000,000 ⭐) to cash out.

🏆 What are the Season Leaderboard Prizes? ▼

Top contenders win huge Coin rewards: 1st: 10,000 🪙, 2nd: 5,000 🪙, 3rd: 2,500 🪙. Qualify by completing at least 100 tasks in the active season!
