📋 Step-by-Step Task Walkthrough
STEP 1

Choose Simple or Premium Task

Simple Tasks (📋) are completely free for all players (earn 50 🪙 each). Premium Tasks (💎) deliver high payouts (earn 200 🪙 each) for VIP members.

STEP 2

Click "GO" & View Mission Target

Tap the GO button to open the mission target (YouTube video, Telegram channel, website, or sponsor link). Keep the page active during the timer countdown.

STEP 3

Click "VERIFY" & Win Instant Coins

Once your mission countdown finishes, tap VERIFY. Anti-cheat validates your visit and instantly credits +50 🪙 or +200 🪙 directly to your wallet!

STEP 4

Subscribe to Premium Tasks

Open the Premium Task tab on the Home page. Choose from 3 discounted plans: 15 Days (500 ⭐), 30 Days (1,000 ⭐), or 3 Months (2,000 ⭐) to unlock high-tier missions.

ADMIN

For Admins: How to Create & Edit Tasks

Go to Profile → Admin Panel → Edit Task → tap ＋ Add Mission. Enter title, reward coins, target URL, and countdown duration, then save!

💡 Frequently Asked Questions
💳 How do I redeem Amazon Shopping Vouchers? ▼

Navigate to the Wallet page in the bottom menu. Select your voucher denomination: ₹1,000 (100,000 ⭐), ₹5,000 (500,000 ⭐), or ₹10,000 (1,000,000 ⭐) to submit your order. Codes are delivered instantly upon approval.

💎 How do Diamonds & Level Unlocks work? ▼

For every 10 completed tasks, you automatically earn +1 Diamond (💎). As your progress fills to 100%, levels unlock automatically (20 Diamonds or 200 tasks auto-unlocks Level 2!).

🏆 What are the Season Leaderboard Rewards? ▼

Top champions win huge coin pools: 1st Place: +10,000 🪙, 2nd Place: +5,000 🪙, 3rd Place: +2,500 🪙. Qualify by completing tasks during the 3-month global season!

🎟️ What are Tickets used for? ▼

Tickets protect your consecutive Daily Streaks! If you ever miss a day, use 200 Tickets to save your streak and avoid the -10 tasks penalty.

⚡ What if a task does not verify? ▼

Make sure you stay on the target page until the countdown finishes. If network issues occur, wait 5 seconds and tap VERIFY again. If you continue experiencing issues, submit a ticket below!
